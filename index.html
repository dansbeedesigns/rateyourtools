Why This Site Exists

Big woodworking tools — table saws, jointers, planers, bandsaws — are rarely on a store floor where you can see them, feel the fence, test the table flatness, or hear how they run under load. You're buying based on spec sheets, marketing photography, and hope.

That's a problem when you're committing $2,000, $3,000, or more. I know this firsthand — my Harvey Alpha table saw is a tool I researched thoroughly and still came away with regrets I only fully understood after owning it. Things no spec sheet told me. Things real owners knew.

RateYourTools.org exists to fill that gap. Real ratings from woodworkers who actually own the tools — no sponsored content, no affiliate pressure — so you can make a confident decision before you spend your hard-earned money.
